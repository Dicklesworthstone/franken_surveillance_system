Alder House synthetic case recordings

Adult visitor and courier
Microsoft Rocketbox, Male_Adult_07 and Delivery_Male_01, with the m_walk_neutral and m_idle_breathe_01 motion captures from the same library. MIT license, reproduced in ROCKETBOX-LICENSE.txt. The showcase converts these assets to glTF, compresses the textures, removes forward translation from the motion cycle, and positions the actors and their hands in the scene.
https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox/tree/0943055db6ec570bcef9f2c8b41c9e5467c808f9

Raccoon
“Low-poly Racoon Run Animation” by avatrass. Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. Obtained through the Objaverse archive. Changes include subdivision of the bind mesh, smooth normals, a new guard coat with a ringed tail and facial mask, scaled anatomy, world-space motion and paw contact. The body and its articulated animation are an artist's reconstruction.
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/ee040f4719fa4b449176f860921a7655
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/objaverse

Sky
