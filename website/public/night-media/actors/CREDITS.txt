Human models
The after-hours visitor uses “Eric Rigged 001 – Rigged 3D Business Man” and the courier uses “Nathan Animated 003 – Walking 3D Man”. Both are by Renderpeople. Renderpeople publishes these two works under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. The recordings depict fictional events.
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/a46bc9f67aaa415bb4f3241eef900e7f
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/143a2b1ea5eb4385ae90a73657aca3bc
https://sketchfab.com/renderpeople
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The web adaptation resizes the photographed colour and tangent-normal maps, tints the visitor’s shirt with the original garment mask, converts the gloss maps for glTF roughness, and binds the bodies to the staged performances. The courier's source includes a captured walking performance. The FBX and source maps used by the build are mirrored in chapter 9 of the Godot 4 Game Development Cookbook at the following revision.
https://github.com/PacktPublishing/Godot-4-Game-Development-Cookbook/tree/e752af943e8cf035def41f17d9efa8e50b1bc135/Chapter%209

Additional human motions
Motion capture from the Microsoft Rocketbox library. MIT license, reproduced in ROCKETBOX-LICENSE.txt. The showcase adapts the motion to the two skinned characters.

Raccoon
“Low-poly Racoon Run Animation” by avatrass. Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. Obtained through the Objaverse archive. Changes include subdivision of the bind mesh, smooth normals, a new guard coat with a ringed tail and facial mask, scaled anatomy, world-space motion and paw contact. The body and its articulated animation are an artist's reconstruction.
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/ee040f4719fa4b449176f860921a7655
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/objaverse

Sky
