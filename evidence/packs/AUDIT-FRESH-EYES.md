## Verdict

**Not publishable as-is.** Two independent critical/high classes must be fixed first: the document
simultaneously asserts the retracted and the corrected boot-gate model (1, 7), and it presents a
never-triggered injection as a delivered root shell while burying the one family (Tapo) that H-008
actually promotes (4, 5, 6). The opcode contradiction (2) and the retracted "no sink" bullet (3) are
one-line fixes but each can mislead a reader into the wrong next test. Citation and hazard gaps (12,
21, 22, 23, 33) mean the doc's two live candidates — AOSU `0x1A` and Wyze env `init=` — carry no
recovery contract and no reachable evidence. With 1-7, 9, 12, 16, 20-23 corrected and pack cites made
resolvable (10, 24, 29-31), the doc is publishable as the research result it claims to be.
